# Code of Conduct

Nictate is a small project, and everyone who takes part in it should be able to do so without harassment. This code
applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space that belongs to the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, and assume good faith. Disagreeing about the product is welcome. Attacking people is not.
- Give feedback that helps. When you report a problem, say what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish other people's private information, including captured text, screenshots, or
  contact details, without their permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or trolling.
- Sexual content, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

To report a conduct problem, open a private security advisory from the repository's
[Security tab](https://github.com/jrthormann/Nictate/security) and say that it concerns conduct. Please don't describe
the problem in a public issue or pull request. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews each report and decides what to do, from a private warning to removing a comment or blocking
a participant. Where it is safe to do so, the maintainer explains the decision to the people involved.
